# Commercial Data Governance

The approved pricing workbook governs only products and package schedules explicitly represented in it.

Products absent from the workbook retain their separately approved commercial schedules. Absence from the workbook does not revoke, replace, deactivate, or otherwise modify those schedules.

Identity corrections must be resolved against the canonical product record before a workbook row is mapped. Alternate names and abbreviations may be retained as search aliases, but package and price authority remains server-side in the canonical ecommerce source and generated Worker catalog.

## MXene direct-order availability

Owner-confirmed 7 September 2026: all eight listed active MXene grades are commercially available through an established, reliable supplier-backed supply chain. Availability is supplier-backed rather than necessarily warehouse-stocked, and normal eligible direct orders are expected to be fulfillable. `Offer.availability` may remain `https://schema.org/InStock` while this condition holds. This does not assert physical inventory in New Jersey or immediate/same-day shipment.

Post-payment fulfillment review covers destination eligibility, logistics, compliance, quantity/order limits, documentation and fulfillment coordination. It is not a speculative per-order check of whether the material can be sourced. Existing visible lead-time, shipping-included and pending-review wording remains applicable.

If Winigen becomes aware that a specific grade cannot be fulfilled in the normal commercial timeframe, that grade must be disabled or its availability updated before further normal direct orders are accepted.

Google Merchant inclusion is derived through the same canonical eligibility pipeline as other products: public checkout status, approved active package pricing, offer eligibility, canonical landing page, and public image. MXene products must not be maintained through family-specific feed exceptions.
