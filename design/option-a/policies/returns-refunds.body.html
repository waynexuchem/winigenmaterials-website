Returns & Refunds

Contact contact@winigenmaterials.com about an order or material concern. Specialty battery chemicals and research materials are generally non-returnable after shipment for change of mind or customer ordering error. This does not limit legally required rights or remedies agreed in your contract.

Incorrect, damaged or nonconforming materials

Winigen will investigate incorrect items or quantities, damage on arrival, verified nonconformance with agreed specifications and other errors attributable to Winigen. Please report visible damage or a wrong shipment within 7 days of delivery; specification concerns within 30 days of delivery, subject to applicable law and contracted rights. Later reports will be reviewed where required by law or the agreed supply terms.

Provide the order number, material name, lot/batch number, delivery date, photographs of the package and labels, and relevant test evidence with methods and conditions where applicable. Preserve packaging and the material safely while the claim is reviewed. Representative analytical results alone do not replace the agreed acceptance specifications.

Never return chemicals without authorization

Do not ship chemicals back without Winigen’s written authorization and safe return instructions. Winigen will confirm whether a return is appropriate, permissible and safe, including packaging, carrier and dangerous-goods requirements. Do not dispose of or reship material on Winigen’s behalf without agreed instructions.

Confirmed claims and shipping costs

For a confirmed claim, Winigen will arrange an appropriate replacement, refund or credit, agreed with the customer and subject to applicable rights. For verified seller error, Winigen will cover reasonable authorized return shipping and replacement shipping; customers should obtain written instructions before incurring costs. A return is not automatically required where unsafe or unnecessary.

Refund processing

Once a refund is approved and any agreed return or verification is complete, Winigen will initiate the refund to the original payment method within 5 business days, and confirm it by email. Bank or payment-provider posting time varies. A credit or alternative payment route will not replace a refund required by law without the customer’s agreement.

Cancellation, nondelivery and inability to supply

Contact Winigen promptly to request cancellation before shipment. Eligibility and any previously agreed, documented nonrecoverable custom-order costs will be reviewed against the quotation, contract and applicable law. If Winigen cannot supply the agreed material, Winigen will contact the customer to agree a revised delivery, an expressly accepted alternative, or cancellation and refund of the unfulfilled portion. No material substitution will be made without agreement. Report nondelivery so Winigen can investigate with the carrier and agree the appropriate remedy. There is no blanket exclusion of refunds for unfulfilled orders.
