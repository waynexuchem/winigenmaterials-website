About this product

TiNbCTₓ is a Ti/Nb-containing bimetallic carbide MXene derived from TiNbAlC. This multilayer powder is specified at 2–20 µm and mixed –O, –OH, –F, –Cl surface terminations. Technical Selection Guide

Why researchers evaluate this grade

Stacked layered morphology provides a starting point for powder processing, conductive composites and electrode research.

Key considerations

Match the powder to the dispersion medium and processing conditions. Consider air and moisture exposure, mixed surface chemistry and the extent of delamination required. Conductivity depends on sample preparation and measurement conditions; listed values should be treated as representative.

How to select between forms

Compare the layer structure, nominal size and preparation route with related grades. Layer count alone does not establish performance; evaluate materials under consistent preparation and measurement conditions.
