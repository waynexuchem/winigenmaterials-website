About this product

Battery-grade methyl butyrate (MB) is a high-purity, low-moisture ester solvent for advanced battery electrolyte research and development. Winigen supplies MB with ≥99.8% purity, <20 ppm water, and <30 ppm acid in research-to-pilot quantities. Direct-order packages are available from 50 g through 1 kg; quantities of 2.5 kg or greater are handled by quotation.
