Ti3C2Tx MXene — Accordion-Structured Multilayer Powder (HF-Etched)

HF etching of Ti₃AlC₂ produces representative accordion-like stacked lamellae. This multilayer grade is offered for technical evaluation of layered-powder processing, interfaces and composite integration; morphology alone does not establish application performance.

The existing LiF/HCl-derived Ti3C2Tx multilayer powder is a separate clay-like grade. Its representative conductivity is 1000 ± 500 S/cm, compared with 10–100 S/cm reported for this HF-etched grade. Measurement conditions are unspecified, so these values are not a controlled performance comparison.

Key specifications

HF etching · Multilayer powder · Dark-purple appearance · Representative conductivity 10–100 S/cm · Specific surface area 15 ± 5 m²/g (method unspecified).
