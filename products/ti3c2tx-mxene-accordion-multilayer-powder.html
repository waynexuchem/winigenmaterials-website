About this product

HF etching of Ti₃AlC₂ produces representative accordion-like stacked lamellae. This multilayer grade is offered for technical evaluation of layered-powder processing, interfaces and composite integration; morphology alone does not establish application performance.

The existing LiF/HCl-derived Ti3C2Tx multilayer powder is a separate clay-like grade. Its representative conductivity is 1000 ± 500 S/cm, compared with 10–100 S/cm reported for this HF-etched grade. Measurement conditions are unspecified, so these values are not a controlled performance comparison.

Key specifications

HF etching · Multilayer powder · Dark-purple appearance · Representative conductivity 10–100 S/cm · Specific surface area 15 ± 5 m²/g (method unspecified).

Product FAQ

Is this the existing LiF/HCl multilayer grade?

No. This is the HF-etched accordion-like multilayer grade from a distinct preparation route; the clay-like LiF/HCl product remains unchanged.

Which technical grade is offered?

Only Grade A is offered: −200 mesh precursor and 2–50 µm nominal post-etching size. Select one of the three approved research package sizes.

Is the surface area a BET result?

The source does not state a measurement method. The 15 ± 5 m²/g value is representative and is not described as BET.

Are purity and conductivity guaranteed?

No purity guarantee or lot-specific result is provided. Conductivity is representative supplier-reported information with unspecified measurement conditions.
