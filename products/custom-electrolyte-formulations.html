    DEFINE THE TECHNICAL QUESTION

    Formulation objectives and tradeoffs

    Low-temperature operation

    Balance transport, wetting and interfacial resistance with stability over the operating range.

    High-voltage compatibility

    Define the cathode, cutoff voltage and formation protocol before comparing cycle behavior.

    Power and fast charge

    Consider polarization, heat generation and electrode compatibility at the target rates.

    Silicon-containing anodes

    Evaluate interphase stability, electrolyte consumption, gas generation and swelling together.

    Sodium-ion systems

    Specify the cathode and hard-carbon pairing, and use matched cycling conditions.

    Components and supply

    Discuss salt, solvent and additive selection, moisture requirements, quantities and packaging.

    DEVELOPMENT & VALIDATION

    From requirements to an evaluation plan

    1. Define the cell

      Set chemistry, temperature, voltage, rates and acceptance criteria.

    2. Select candidates

      Agree on reference conditions and a suitable screening scope.

    3. Review the evidence

      Compare measured performance, methods, limitations and tradeoffs.

    4. Plan supply

      Discuss refinement, quantities, packaging and the next development stage.

    Testing responsibilities, partner involvement and manufacturing or supply scope are agreed for each program.
