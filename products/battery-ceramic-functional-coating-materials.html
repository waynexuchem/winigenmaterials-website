Applications

Where These Materials Fit

Winigen Materials supplies boehmite and alumina powders and specialty ceramic morphologies for the gap between laboratory formulation and manufacturing process validation.

  • Separator and electrode ceramic coating
  • Electrode edge-insulation trials
  • Thermal-stability and shrinkage-control layers
  • Local slurry preparation with the customer's selected binder and solvent route
  • FAT and pilot coating-line material trials

Documentation

U.S.-facing technical and quality support

Available documentation may include Winigen-format TDS, lot-specific COA when available, SDS, storage and handling notes, dispersion guidance, and NDA-supported technical discussion.

Can Winigen support FAT or pilot-line trials?

Yes. Requests can be scoped around coating method, trial quantity, substrate, local mixing capability, drying conditions, and the documentation required for process evaluation.

Why are powder grades preferred?

Powder generally offers better international-shipping and storage robustness while allowing the receiving team to select its binder, solvent, solids content, and rheology locally.
