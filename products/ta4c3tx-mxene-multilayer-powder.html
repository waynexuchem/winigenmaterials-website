    About this product

    Ta₄C₃Tₓ belongs to the M₄C₃-type carbide composition family and is derived from Ta₄AlC₃. This multilayer powder is specified at 2–20 µm and mixed –O, –OH, –F, –Cl surface terminations. Compare the preparation route, layer form and processing requirements when selecting a grade. Representative characterization and a technical data sheet are provided for evaluation; lot-specific documentation can be discussed during technical review.

    Technical Selection Guide

    Why researchers evaluate this grade

    Stacked layered morphology provides a starting point for powder processing, conductive composites and electrode research.

    Key considerations

    Match the powder to the dispersion medium and processing conditions. Consider air and moisture exposure, mixed surface chemistry and the extent of delamination required. Conductivity depends on sample preparation and measurement conditions; listed values should be treated as representative.

    How to select between forms

    Compare the layer structure, nominal size and preparation route with related grades. Layer count alone does not establish performance; evaluate materials under consistent preparation and measurement conditions.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How does multilayer differ from single-/few-layer?

    Multilayer powder retains stacked layered structure. Select the form according to your dispersion, processing and interface requirements.

    Are the surface terminations fixed?

    Surface termination distributions vary with synthesis and lot. The listed terminations do not establish fixed proportions or confirm current-lot quantitative surface analysis. Discuss any required analysis before ordering.
