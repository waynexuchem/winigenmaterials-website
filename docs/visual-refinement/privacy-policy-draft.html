Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Winigen Materials LLC (“Winigen,” “we,” “us”) handles personal information in connection with our website, material inquiries, quotations, orders and related communications. It applies to people using the website and people acting for customers or prospective customers. It does not replace a separate confidentiality or supply agreement.

Who is responsible and how to contact us

Winigen Materials LLC is responsible for the website and our use of customer and inquiry information. Contact contact@winigenmaterials.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line, or write to Winigen Materials LLC, 24 Park Ave, PO Box 116, Park Ridge, NJ 07656, USA. Please do not send payment-card numbers, passwords or sensitive personal information in an inquiry.

Information you provide

Our inquiry form collects your name, email address, company if supplied, inquiry type, material or product interest, quantity range and message. The form also records the source page and form location to help us understand and route the request. Messages and direct emails may contain technical requirements, correspondence and supporting information you choose to share. Share only the information needed for your request and avoid confidential formulations unless an appropriate agreement is in place.

For quotations and orders, we process product and package selections, quantities, prices, currency, order references, contact details, and relevant billing and delivery information. Payment processing returns payment status and transaction references used to reconcile an order. Order records include material line items, amounts, destination information and fulfillment or notification status. We also retain communications needed to handle shipping, documentation, claims, cancellations and refunds.

Information from website use

Website delivery and security services process technical information such as IP addresses, browser and device information, requested URLs, timestamps, referring pages and security signals. We use these services to deliver pages, prevent abuse, diagnose failures and maintain the website. Our inquiry flow uses Cloudflare Turnstile to check for automated or abusive submissions, together with a request token and duplicate-submission controls. These checks are separate from accepting a material inquiry.

Cart storage, cookies and analytics

The website uses browser local storage to remember cart items and package quantities, the selected shipping country, checkout snapshots and certain review or order-routing state. Session storage supports temporary navigation or checkout state and the current featured-material selection. A transaction-specific local marker helps avoid repeated purchase-event dispatch. This browser storage remains until removed, replaced by the website or cleared by your browser; it does not establish that an order has been paid.

On the production website, Google Analytics 4 measures website use and ecommerce activity. The Google tag is initialized when a production page is opened. Google may use cookies and similar identifiers and receive page addresses, referral information, browser/device details and interaction information. Purchase measurement includes an order transaction ID, merchandise value, currency and purchased item identifiers, names, package information, prices and quantities, with tax or shipping amounts where applicable. We do not intentionally include your name, email address or payment-card information in the purchase analytics payload. Order or session references may also be present in technical page addresses used during checkout navigation.

Our current Analytics property permits advertising personalization and granular location/device collection; Google signals is not enabled. Permission in these settings is not proof that a personalized advertising campaign is running. Eligible analytics information may be used through linked advertising services according to their configuration and applicable consent requirements.

You can restrict cookies or storage using your browser settings, clear locally saved data, use content-blocking tools or use Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on where supported. Restricting storage can affect cart persistence and checkout continuity. The current site does not have an on-page analytics preference centre. Do not assume that closing a page deletes information already sent to a service provider. See how Google uses information from partner sites for Google’s practices and available controls.

How we use information

  • Respond to inquiries, evaluate requirements, prepare quotations and provide requested technical documents.
  • Accept and reconcile payments; administer orders, delivery, fulfillment review, claims and refunds.
  • Send operational acknowledgments, order notifications and relevant customer communications.
  • Maintain business and accounting records, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes and prevent fraud or misuse.
  • Understand website and purchase activity and improve navigation, documentation and services.

We do not add a website visitor to an email newsletter simply because they browse or buy. You may ask us to stop nonessential follow-up communications; we may still send messages needed to administer an order, resolve a request or meet a legal obligation.

Service providers and other disclosures

We use Cloudflare for website delivery, security, request handling and supporting services, including database and queue services used by the inquiry and commerce workflows. We use Stripe’s hosted Checkout for payment collection. Payment-card entry occurs on Stripe’s hosted payment page rather than in Winigen’s inquiry form. Stripe processes payment and billing information and may process device, fraud-prevention and transaction information under its own terms and privacy notices. Our server uses verified payment notifications to update order state; a browser redirect alone is not proof of payment.

Resend provides transactional email delivery for configured inquiry and order notifications. Email addresses, message content and delivery information are processed to send and track those communications. We may share necessary contact, delivery and shipment information with carriers, logistics providers and supply partners to fulfill the agreed order. Google processes analytics information as described above. We may disclose information to professional advisers, authorities or other parties where reasonably necessary for legal obligations, business protection, a dispute, or a business transaction, with appropriate restrictions where applicable.

We do not sell inquiry or order information for monetary payment. This statement does not describe every legal definition of “sale,” “sharing” or targeted advertising; the use and configuration of analytics services require separate assessment under applicable privacy laws. Links to third-party services, including Stripe and LinkedIn, lead to sites governed by their own notices. Provider information: Cloudflare, Stripe, Resend, and Google.

Retention and security

We keep inquiry, quotation, order and correspondence records for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which they were collected, ongoing customer relationships, legal and accounting duties, claims, fraud prevention and dispute resolution. Retention depends on the record and its purpose; this policy does not promise a universal automatic deletion deadline. Service providers retain information under their applicable arrangements and notices. Browser storage can be removed using browser controls.

As reviewed on November 12, 2026, Google Analytics is configured for 2 months of event-data retention and 14 months of user-data retention, with the user-data period reset on new activity. These settings do not set a universal deletion deadline for aggregated reports, Winigen order records, correspondence or other service-provider records.

We use safeguards designed to reduce unauthorized access, loss or misuse. Access and handling should be limited to people and providers who need the information for the relevant purpose. No website, email system or method of storage can be guaranteed completely secure.

International processing

Winigen operates from the United States. Providers and business partners may process information in the United States and other countries in which they operate. Local privacy laws and available protections may differ. Provider privacy notices describe their international processing and transfer arrangements.

Your requests and choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or a copy of your personal information, to restrict or object to certain processing, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Contact us using the details above and explain your request. We may need information to verify your identity and authority, and may retain records where a legal or other permitted exception applies. We will handle requests within the periods required by applicable law. You may also have the right to contact the relevant privacy regulator.

Where a law requires us to identify a legal basis, processing may be necessary to respond to a requested quotation or carry out a contract, comply with legal obligations, pursue legitimate business interests such as security and customer support, or rely on consent where required. A right or legal basis depends on the activity and applicable law; this notice is not a claim that every privacy regime applies to every visitor.

Children and changes

The website is intended for business and technical users and is not directed to children. We do not seek personal information from children. Contact us if you believe a child has provided personal information. We may update this policy as our practices change; the published policy will identify its effective date and material updates as appropriate.
